# Echoes: privacy policy

*Last updated: 4 November 2026*

Echoes is a game. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other
personal information, it shows no ads, and it has no in-app purchases.

## What stays on your phone
The game saves your runs (the path your bot flew and your score), your best score per board,
a random callsign it made up for you, and your sound setting. These live only in the app's
storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

## What leaves your phone
Nothing. This version of the app does not send any data anywhere.

(If a shared leaderboard is added later, this policy will be updated first. It would send only
your random callsign, your scores and saved runs, and an anonymous sign-in ID, so others can see
the board.)

## Children
Echoes is not directed at children under 13.
