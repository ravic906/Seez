**Full description** (4000 max):

Your ship is gone. You are the one rescue bot that got out.

Slide your thumb to thread the falling wreckage. Every run you fly comes back as a crew ghost,
still flying your old route, so the better you get, the harder your past gets to escape.

- One-thumb controls, made for short sessions.
- Your ghosts: your best run and your last flight return to chase you, on a schedule set by
  the board.
- Alien snails throw hammers, jacks and bombs. Watch them shake, then slide away.
- Ten helpers: Nitro, Shield, Slow-mo, Wide gaps, Foresight, Exorcise, Shrink, Gold rush,
  Repair and Phase. Better ones appear the deeper you go.
- A new daily board for everyone, or a fresh random board any time.
- Three suit cells per run. Wreckage ends the run at once.
- No ads, no purchases, no account, no personal data.
